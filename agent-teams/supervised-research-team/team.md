A reusable team for literature, theoretical, computational and empirical research.
Start with the Research Supervisor: give it the research question, your materials, the
workspace and any limits on access or cost. An unfinished study is a valid input.
You do not need to be a researcher: the team owns how the research is done.

## Responsibilities

- **Research Supervisor** owns intake, the user's goal, the research direction, the
  method review including its own direction, independent review of the evidence, each
  assignment and the final assessment.
- **Researcher** owns the study of existing work, investigation, methods, experiments or
  proofs, analysis, reproducible artifacts and any requested manuscript. It proposes
  conclusions; it cannot complete the study.

## Cooperation

The supervisor assigns, the researcher reports, and the supervisor reviews and decides,
until the study is complete or needs the user. Both keep the research map current: the
study's one living document of goal, knowledge, progress and decisions. The shared
[research contract](shared/research-contract.md) holds the evidence standard, the study
records, statuses and runtime limits; each member follows its attached skill, and the
[configuration](team-config.json) owns the handoffs. A terminal assessment returns to
the user or caller, or to a containing package's matching route; no parent package is
required. External publication is not part of this team.
