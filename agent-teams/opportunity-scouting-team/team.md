The user often notices that something is inconvenient and moves on. This team takes such a thought, researches it, and says whether it is a product worth building quickly.

## Members

- `opportunity_scout`: the entry point. Owns the user's thoughts, the research questions, the judgement of existing solutions and demand, the opportunity brief, the ranked backlog, and the hand-off of approved opportunities.
- `deep_researcher`: the shared Deep Researcher. Researches the scout's questions from primary sources and returns a sourced research brief.

## Where things live

Everything is kept in the user's opportunities workspace, outside any product repository: the thoughts as given, one folder per opportunity with its research and brief, and the ranked backlog.
