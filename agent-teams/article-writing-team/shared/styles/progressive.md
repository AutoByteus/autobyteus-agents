# Progressive

The reader is led step by step from what they already know to the point. Example: an article on a self-improving agent platform that starts from how a person learns by doing.

- **Reader:** does not yet hold the article's concepts; `brief.md` lists what they do hold.
- **Opening:** the question and the route; the answer comes where its concepts exist (principles, the foundation). The product, its parts and their status come where the implementation begins.
- **Order:** from something the reader knows (a behavior, a familiar problem) to the concepts drawn from it, the model, its mapping to engineering, and only then the implementation (principles, section 4, "A derivation builds before it names").
- **Sentences:** plain and short; a new term only after what it rests on.
- **The page:** a figure where the road takes a step the reader must picture; later figures build on the earlier ones.
- **Review:** the cold read marks every word the reader cannot place where they meet it.
