# Essay

An argument, opinion or strategic viewpoint, only when the user asks for one.

- **Reader:** as the brief says.
- **Opening:** the thesis in the first paragraph; a grounded contrast may open (writing principles, section 1; explanatory writing, section 1).
- **Order:** the argument, its evidence and the strongest objection answered.
- **Sentences:** medium to high rhetorical pressure, every claim still grounded.
- **The page:** text-led; a figure only where an idea earns one.
- **Review:** the thesis is stated once and every step of the argument holds.
